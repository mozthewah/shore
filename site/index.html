Features

Everything on one quiet screen.

Every logo drawn in one style, a picture you choose, and nothing that shouts. Your remote's Home button goes straight to Shore, and holding it still opens Google TV.

Three layouts

Dock and List draw every logo in one consistent style. Shelf is an Apple TV–style row of banners, with a Top Shelf of what's trending in the focused app.

HDMI devices, by name

Switch on a console and "PlayStation 5" appears, named over HDMI-CEC. Shore can ask to switch to it. Switch it off and the tile goes away.

  • PlayStation 5
  • Apple TV
  • HDMI 3

Weather, and weather effects

The temperature and sky in °C or °F, with no key or account. Turn on effects and rain, snow, fog or a storm falls over your photo when it's happening outside.

9 °C Rain

Beautiful wallpapers

Fifteen photographs with pre-rendered glass, NASA, Bing, Wikimedia and Unsplash online, animated loops, and your own photos from your phone. Mark favourites, hide the rest.

The music sets the scene

What's playing in Spotify or any app shows under the clock, and its album art becomes the background until the music stops.

Headlines you choose

One headline at a time under the clock and on the screensaver. Google News for your region, an outlet in your language, or any RSS feed.

Its own screensaver

Your photos drift slowly by, alone or as a mosaic, with a big clock, the weather and the song. Night mode dims everything on a schedule, with calmer photos after dark.

Your remote, your rules

Home opens Shore. Reassign the app shortcut keys or the TV button to an app, an input or the screensaver.

Eight open fonts

Manrope, Inter, Geist, Sora, Outfit and more, with text weight, name and icon size, and four clock sizes.

Speaks your language

English, German, French, Spanish, Dutch, Polish and Ukrainian, following the TV's system language.

Under the clock

The time, and a little more.

Glance up from the sofa and see the weather, what's playing and the news, in small, calm type under the clock. Each one is on its own settings page, and each one can be switched off.

Screensaver

When nobody's watching.

After a few quiet minutes, Shore fades to its own screensaver instead of Google's.

  • Always moving. Every photo slowly pans and zooms, sometimes alone, sometimes as a mosaic of two or three.
  • Your photos. Your wallpapers, online sources and the photos you sent from your phone.
  • The clock, the weather, the song and the news. They move to another corner every few minutes, so nothing burns in.
  • Gets out of the way. Any button brings Home back. After four hours the TV's own sleep timer takes over.

Top Shelf

What's on, before you open the app.

In the Shelf layout, the focused app fills the screen with what's trending there. Press down for a row of picks, then OK to jump straight in.

  • YouTube, Twitch, ARD, ZDF and ARTE open the exact video or stream.
  • Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video and more show trending titles from TMDB.
  • Fetched about once an hour, over HTTPS only.

Phone companion

Your phone, as Shore's settings app.

Turn on Settings › Phone and scan the code. A small web app opens on your phone, served by the TV itself.

  • Send photos, GIFs and short videos. Frame each photo in the TV's 16:9 shape first; they become wallpapers under My photos.
  • Every TV setting, from your phone. The same groups as on the TV, plus the apps on Home and their order.
  • Weather place and news feed. Type the city or paste an RSS address instead of typing on the remote.
  • Backup and restore. Save all your settings to the phone and bring them back, or onto another TV.
  • Private by design. It runs only on your Wi-Fi, needs the paired code, and nothing goes through the internet.

A closer look

Made for the sofa.

Big type, clear focus and calm motion. Every screen works with the remote's arrows, OK and Back.

Install

Ten minutes, fully reversible.

Shore installs next to Google TV, not instead of it. After the first install it updates itself from GitHub releases, with no computer needed.

  1. Turn on developer mode

    Settings › System › About, then press OK on Android TV OS build seven times.

  2. Allow USB or network debugging

    In Developer options, then connect from a computer with adb connect <tv-ip>.

  3. Install the APK

    Download it from Releases and run adb install -r with the file.

  4. Make Home open Shore

    Turn on Shore Home button in Settings › System › Accessibility.

The README has the full guide, including Android 14 wireless debugging and an optional, reversible debloat list.

Questions

Good to know.

Can I go back to the normal Google TV home screen?

Always. Shore installs next to Google TV and changes nothing on its own. Hold Home for a second to open Google TV's Home, turn off the Shore Home button in Accessibility, or uninstall Shore and the TV is exactly as before.

What does Shore send over the internet?

Only what a feature you turned on needs: the weather from Open-Meteo, headlines from the news source you chose, online wallpapers from the sources you ticked, Top Shelf picks, and, if you allow it, the artist and title to find album art. No accounts, no analytics, no ads. The phone companion stays on your home Wi-Fi.

Which TVs does it work on?

Shore is made and tested on a TCL 55C6KS with Google TV (Android 12). It is a regular Android TV app, so other Google TV and Android TV devices should work, but they haven't been tested.

How do updates work?

Settings › Google TV › Shore updates checks the public releases on GitHub, automatically if you like, and installs a new version after you confirm. Your settings and photos stay.

Is it really free?

Yes. Shore is open source under the MIT licence, based on CleanFrame by Nathan Clark. Fonts, photos and other third-party parts keep their own licences, listed in the repository.
