A calm home screen
for Google TV.

Shore swaps the busy Google TV home screen for the things you actually use: your apps, your HDMI devices, a beautiful picture and the time. Nothing else.

Download the APK View on GitHub

Free and open source (MIT) · No account · No ads · Tested on a TCL 55C6KS

Features

Everything on one quiet screen.

Every logo drawn in one style. A picture you choose. Your remote's Home button takes you straight there, and holding it still opens Google TV when you need it.

Three layouts

Dock and List draw every logo in one consistent style. Shelf is an Apple TV–style row of banners with a Top Shelf of what's trending.

HDMI devices, by name

Switch on a console and "PlayStation 5" appears, named over HDMI-CEC. Switch it off and the tile goes away.

Beautiful wallpapers

Fifteen photographs with pre-rendered glass, NASA, Bing and Unsplash online, animated loops, and your own photos from your phone.

Under the clock

The weather in °C or °F, with no key or account needed, and what's playing in Spotify, YouTube or any other app.

Phone companion

Scan a code and send photos, GIFs and clips to the TV from a little web app that looks like the launcher.

Your remote, your rules

Home opens Shore. Reassign the app shortcut keys or the TV button to an app, an input or the screensaver.

Night mode and idle

Dims everything on a schedule. After a minute of quiet, only the picture and the clock stay.

Eight open fonts

Manrope, Inter, Geist, Sora, Outfit and more, with text weight and size, plus four clock sizes.

Speaks your language

English, German, French, Spanish, Dutch and Polish, following the TV's system language.

Top Shelf

What's on, before you open the app.

In the Shelf layout, the focused app fills the screen with what's trending there. Press down for a row of picks, then OK to jump straight in.

  • YouTube, Twitch, ARD, ZDF and ARTE open the exact video or stream
  • Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video and more show trending titles from TMDB
  • Fetched about once an hour, over HTTPS only

Phone companion

Your photos on the big screen.

Turn on Settings › Phone and scan the code. A small web app opens on your phone, served by the TV itself.

  • Send photos, GIFs and short videos. They become wallpapers, with the same frosted glass as the built-in ones.
  • Set the weather place without typing on the remote.
  • Add it to your home screen and it opens full screen, like an app.
  • Private by design. It runs only on your Wi-Fi, needs the paired code, and nothing goes through the internet.

A closer look

Made for the sofa.

Big type, clear focus and calm motion. Every screen works with the remote's arrows, OK and Back.

Install

Ten minutes, fully reversible.

Shore installs next to Google TV, not instead of it. Uninstall it and your TV is exactly as before.

  1. Turn on developer mode

    Settings › System › About, then press OK on Android TV OS build seven times.

  2. Allow USB or network debugging

    In Developer options, then connect from a computer with adb connect <tv-ip>.

  3. Install the APK

    Download it from Releases and run adb install -r with the file.

  4. Make Home open Shore

    Turn on Shore Home button in Settings › System › Accessibility.

The README has the full guide, including Android 14 wireless debugging and an optional, reversible debloat list.
